# CancerLess

## Prevent Cancer Before It Starts

CancerLess is a cancer prevention and early detection platform designed to help individuals understand cancer risks, adopt healthier habits, access evidence-based screening guidance, and receive educational support through innovative digital tools.

### Features

- General Cancer Risk Assessment
- Breast Cancer Risk Assessment
- Prostate Cancer Risk Assessment
- Lung Cancer Risk Assessment
- Colorectal Cancer Risk Assessment
- Daily Prevention Habit Tracking
- Weekly Health Check-ins
- Eddie AI Educational Assistant
- Personalized Prevention Guidance

### Mission

CancerLess exists to reduce cancer risk through prevention, education, screening awareness, and healthy lifestyle choices.
